# Unified Matter closure loop

This tranche treats controversy, proof obligations, investigation, reviewed evidence and selective reopening as coordinates of one persisted Matter world rather than mutually exclusive product routes.

Normal product entry remains `SENSIBLAW_MATTER_SCOPE`. A scope may now carry persisted `controversy_refs`; the Generic Matter loader reopens each controversy under the same `matter_ref` and, for every open proof obligation, reopens any controversy-owned investigation/evidence/reassessment history from SLR.

The visible closure surface keeps these distinctions explicit:

- typed disagreement is not Boolean negation;
- requested discriminator is not evidence;
- Pareto-frontier membership is not recommendation;
- blocked route is not a bad route;
- acquisition is not review;
- reviewed evidence is not proposition truth;
- selective reopening is not residual resolution;
- evidence supplied does not determine credibility, ultimate fact or legal authority.

The older `ITIR_CONTROVERSY_REF` and `ITIR_INV_ACQUISITION_REF` selectors remain compatibility/deep-link modes. When a Matter scope is present it is the composition root and takes precedence, so specialist selectors cannot split the user's active semantic world.

The SLR dependency is pinned to the exact EXTERNAL-CLOSURE-1 head consumed by this branch. The lockfile and full production-data execution remain exact-head verification obligations; no compile/live-Postgres green claim is made by this document.
